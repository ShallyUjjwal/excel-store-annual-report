# 🛍️ Store Annual Report 2022 (Excel Dashboard)

This project is a comprehensive Excel dashboard designed to analyze and visualize the **annual performance** of a retail store. It provides valuable insights into sales trends, customer demographics, product categories, and regional performance.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Order Trends**: Monthly comparison of total sales vs order volume
- **Demographics**: Sales split by gender and age group
- **Category Filters**: Dynamic slicers for product categories, months, and sales channels
- **Top Regions**: Sales performance across top 5 Indian states
- **Sales Channels**: Distribution across platforms like Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, etc.
- **Order Status**: Breakdown of delivered, returned, cancelled, and refunded orders

---

## 📌 Final Recommendation

> Target **women customers aged 30–49** in **Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh**, by running campaigns and offers on **Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra**.
